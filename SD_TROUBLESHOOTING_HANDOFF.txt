Observed Results So Far
1) SD card media preparation on Mac is good
- Card was reformatted as a single FAT32 partition and mounted as LOOTR.
- 112 .raw files were copied to SD root successfully.

2) Firmware upload to Teensy works
- Production and diagnostics variants compile and upload.

3) Runtime SD detection problem remains
- Boot output from embedded firmware showed:
  - "Lootr booting..."
  - "ERROR: SD card not found. Check wiring/card."
- Therefore failure is at SD initialization (SD.begin), before asset enumeration.

4) Pin-number understanding confirmed
- Teensy board numeric labels correspond directly to digital pin numbers in code.
- Example: board pin 10 equals D10 in firmware.

5) Electrical checks performed by user
- Continuity checks passed for intended connections.
- MISO observed around 0.5-0.9 V on DC multimeter and unstable.

Interpretation Notes
- A DC multimeter reading on SPI data lines can be misleading because:
  - SPI is fast digital switching and the meter averages pulses.
  - MISO can be tri-stated/floating when idle depending on module/card state.
- So 0.5-0.9 V on MISO does not alone prove failure.

Likely Causes (ranked)
1) Module power expectation mismatch
- Many SD reader breakouts with onboard 3.3 V regulator expect 5 V at VCC.
- If powered from 3.3 V when designed for 5 V input, onboard path may be underpowered.

2) Intermittent mechanical contact despite continuity spot-checks
- Flimsy Dupont jumpers can pass static continuity but fail under vibration/position.

3) CS or SPI line instability under load
- Especially CS and GND contact quality.

Recommended Next Steps
1) Run diagnostics firmware continuously while manipulating wires
- Flash:
  - ./scripts/flash_sd_diag.sh
- Monitor:
  - ./scripts/monitor.sh teensy40_sd_diag
- Expectation:
  - Stable wiring: repeated [PASS ...] lines
  - Flaky wiring: [FAIL ...] spikes when touched/moved

2) Verify exact wiring mapping again
- SD CS -> Teensy 10
- SD SCK -> Teensy 13
- SD MOSI -> Teensy 11
- SD MISO -> Teensy 12
- SD GND -> Teensy GND
- SD VCC -> currently 3.3 V (see step 3)

3) Power-input test for module expectation
- Try SD module VCC at 5 V (keep logic lines unchanged) only if module is a 5 V-input breakout.
- Re-run diagnostics and compare PASS/FAIL stability.
- Safety check: verify MISO never exceeds 3.3 V relative to GND.

4) Prefer robust physical connections
- Shorter wires, firmer headers, avoid loose Dupont jumpers.
- Re-seat SD card and all jumpers.

5) If available, use logic analyzer/scope
- Verify real SPI signaling on CS/SCK/MOSI/MISO during SD.begin attempts.
